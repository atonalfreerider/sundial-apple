# NASA Blue Marble Earth texture — App Review provenance

`Apps/Resources/earth_texture.png` is the same color-adjusted derivative used by the Android app
of NASA Visible Earth's 2002 **The Blue Marble: Land Surface, Ocean Color and Sea Ice** map.

- Canonical source: https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57730/land_ocean_ice_2048.png
- Source collection: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/collection/1484/blue-marble
