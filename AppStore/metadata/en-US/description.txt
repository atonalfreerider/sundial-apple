Sundial tells time the way the sky does. The year is a dial around the Sun, the day is a dial around the Earth, and the Moon keeps its own month — all computed from real orbital elements and drawn as one precise, beautiful instrument.

THE SOLAR VIEW
• The year as a sprocket dial: every day, every Sunday and every month, with the solstices and equinoxes on the season cross.
• Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars in their true positions, each trailing its orbit.
• The Earth carries its own subdial — a 24-hour gear with an enlarged Moon on its track.
• Seasons shade the dial and fade into one another at each solstice and equinox.

THE EARTH VIEW
• Tap the Sun and fly in to the Earth.
• A lit, textured globe turns in real sidereal time, with its axis tilting toward the Sun in June and away in December.
• Noon faces the Sun on a 24-hour ring; the local wheel points to your time zone's hour, and shows where the new date has already begun.
• The lunar dial marks each coming midnight where the Moon will be, so you can read the phase ahead.

THE GALACTIC VIEW
• Watch the Sun carry the planets through space, their orbits drawn out into helices, and slide through the years without end.

YOUR CALENDAR ON THE DIALS
• Show events from the calendars on your iPhone: long events around the year, today's events around the day.
• Read-only and private: calendar data never leaves your device.

HOME SCREEN, LOCK SCREEN AND APPLE WATCH
• Widgets put the whole instrument on your Home Screen, in the solar view or the Earth view, and the year, the Moon and the season on your Lock Screen.
• On Apple Watch, the instrument fits your wrist: turn the Digital Crown to move through time, fly to the Earth, or leave the Solar System for the galactic view. Touch and hold the dial for settings; the instrument stays on the always-on display.
• Complications bring the year dial and the Moon to any watch face.

AESTHETICS
• Void Black, Crimson Nebula, Deep Space Blue, Cosmic Violet, Solar Bronze — and Brass Watch, which engraves the whole instrument in polished brass.

OPTIONAL ASTROLOGY MODE
• Zodiac ring, planetary symbols (☿ ♀ ⊕ ♂ ☽) and the signs they fall in.
• A daily horoscope written privately on your device by Apple Intelligence, where available. Readings are for entertainment, and any reading can be reported from inside the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads. Your calendar and birth details stay on your device.
